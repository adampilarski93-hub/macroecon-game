# Plurinational Path Thinkers

## Purpose

Guide integration of Latin American political thought into the Plurinational Path scenario. Use alongside the south-american-socialism-design skill (Prashad, Bolivia, Brazil, Venezuela, Chile).

## Thinker Catalog

### José Carlos Mariátegui (Peru, 1894–1930)
- **Key work**: *Seven Interpretative Essays on Peruvian Reality* (1928)
- **Insight**: The "Indian problem" is not humanitarian but socio-economic — rooted in land (latifundium) and feudal structures. Indigenous communities are central to the class struggle, not objects of charity.
- **Use for**: Indigenous/land nodes, worker-peasant alliance, plurinational unity

### Salvador Allende (Chile, 1908–1973)
- **Context**: Elected president 1970; overthrown by Pinochet coup 1973
- **Insight**: "Via Chilena" — democratic road to socialism through elections, nationalisation (copper), land reform. Showed that constitutional change invites imperial retaliation.
- **Use for**: Resource nationalism, coup threat, democratic socialism under pressure

### Fidel Castro (Cuba, 1926–2016)
- **Insight**: Solidarity as practice, not rhetoric. Latin American problems require action, not conferences. Cuba supported anti-imperialist struggles across the hemisphere.
- **Use for**: Regional solidarity, alternative partners, resistance to U.S. pressure

### Bolivia's MAS (Movimiento al Socialismo)
- **Context**: Emerged from coca growers; Evo Morales 2005–2019
- **Insight**: Plurinational unity as platform — communitarianism, indigenismo, socialism of the 21st century. Party built from social movements; hybrid grassroots/top-down organisation.
- **Use for**: Plurinational constitution, Indigenous representation, movement-party tension

### Raúl Prebisch (Argentina, 1901–1986)
- **Key work**: ECLA/CEPAL; Prebisch–Singer thesis (1949)
- **Insight**: Center-periphery — terms of trade favour the industrial "center" and exploit the commodity "periphery." Import-substituting industrialisation (ISI) to break dependence.
- **Use for**: Diversification, extractivism, World Bank/IMF conditionalities, uneven development

### Augusto Sandino (Nicaragua, 1895–1934)
- **Context**: Led resistance to U.S. occupation 1927–1933
- **Insight**: National sovereignty against Monroe Doctrine. Peasants, workers, Indigenous fighters in one army. Abolish imperial doctrine; continental solidarity.
- **Use for**: Anti-imperialism, sovereignty, U.S. pressure, armed vs. institutional resistance

### Hugo Blanco (Peru, 1934–2023)
- **Context**: Peasant movement leader; land occupations; CCP (Campesino Confederation)
- **Insight**: Organise peasant unions first; strikes can paralyse estates that depend on unpaid Indigenous labour. Land reform through mass mobilisation, not guerrilla foco alone.
- **Use for**: Land reform, labor-peasant alliance, Indigenous territorial struggle

### Roger Merino (Peru, contemporary)
- **Key work**: *Socio-Legal Struggles for Indigenous Self-Determination in Latin America* (2021)
- **Insight**: Extractivism conflicts stem from colonial patterns and differing political ontologies — Indigenous peoples vs. state. Indigenous territorial defence = politics of self-determination; recognise nations with territorial rights, not ethnic minorities with property rights.
- **Use for**: Extractivism vs. Indigenous territory, FPIC, plurinational state design

### Romina Green Rioja & Nayla Luz Vacarezza (contemporary)
- **Context**: Feminist scholars; plurinational feminist movements
- **Insight**: "Somos Pluri" — plurinationalism as diversity within movements. Continental feminist network committed to plurinationalism; collective struggles of women and gender-diverse people across Abya Yala.
- **Use for**: Plurinational unity, movement diversity, gender and Indigenous intersection

## Integration Rules

1. **Plain language**: One thinker per paragraph max; explain the insight.
2. **Pattern**: `Mariátegui argued that...` or `As Prebisch showed, the terms of trade...`
3. **Match theme to thinker**:
   - Land/Indigenous → Mariátegui, Blanco, Merino
   - Resource nationalism/coups → Allende, Sandino
   - Diversification/ISI → Prebisch
   - Plurinational platform → MAS, Green Rioja & Vacarezza
   - Solidarity → Castro, Sandino
   - Extractivism vs. territory → Merino, Blanco

## Anti-Patterns

- Don't reduce thinkers to slogans; use their actual arguments.
- Merino: Indigenous peoples are nations, not ethnic minorities.
- Green Rioja & Vacarezza: Plurinationalism includes gender diversity.
